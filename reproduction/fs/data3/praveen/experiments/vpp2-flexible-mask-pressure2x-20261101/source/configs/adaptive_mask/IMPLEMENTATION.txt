SCOPE
One seed (100008), 30,000 fitting windows, 1,119 development windows, 35 task
 groups. Balanced per-task sampling without replacement within each task.
All pretrained video, action and encoder weights remain frozen. Train 5,120 shared
hidden scores, 539,136 layer-specific FFN scores and 1,560 layer-specific conditioning
attention head scores: 545,816 scores total. No video-token or action-expert pruning.
All 40 self-attention K/V slots retain the original 40 x 128 output geometry.

MASK FORWARD / BACKWARD
Predictions use binary selections throughout. A fixed-temperature sigmoid straight-
through estimator supplies approximate gradients. This is not an exact derivative of
the discrete architecture decision. Disabled channels remain present and can regrow.
Mask-aware normalization excludes removed coordinates, matching physical slicing.
Only final export deletes tensors. Dense mask search itself is not a compact forward.
FFNs have per-layer floors of 4,096 and retain packs of 128 ranked coordinates;
conditioning attention retains at least eight complete heads per eligible layer.
Layer indices 0–38 have local masks. Layer 39 retains its full internal FFN/head
counts, but shared hidden-width selection applies throughout the residual pathway.

SUPERVISION
GT action flow = 1.0, with native valid-step and time weighting.
Teacher future-video flow = 0.05 x reliability x ramp, planes 3 and 4 only.
Teacher K/V = 0.05 x reliability x ramp, layer indices 20–39 only.
Teacher action imitation, soft-budget, entropy and discreteness losses = 0.
All three active objectives update mask scores, including hidden scores. A larger
scalar coefficient does not guarantee a larger gradient contribution.
Teacher and candidate have matched observations, instruction, robot state and noise.
The original and masked passes share immutable weights, not intermediate states.
Gradients flow through the frozen action expert to candidate masks.
Reliability = clip(1 - teacher_GT_flow_error / max(zero_flow_error,1e-8),0,1),
with detached student/zero/component vetoes. The same weight gates both auxiliaries.
No reliability calibration pass. Rejected examples still have GT action supervision
and remain in all quality measurements. This proxy does not certify video correctness
or rollout success. The teacher-loss ramp spans 100 actually-pruned updates.

CONDITIONAL HIDDEN SEARCH
Widths: 5,120 -> 4,992 -> 4,864, with no mandatory removal. Shared channel identities
are learned; the quality controller controls the count. The 5% floor is a search
bound, not a claim of safety. No independent hidden selections across layers.
Start a trial only after a complete accepted development check (at or after update
280, no later than 1,120), complete passing fitting feedback, bounded channel churn,
active score gradients, and three monitoring observations with >=99.5% overlap of
the proposed retained hidden identities (adjacent and first-to-last).
At most two transitions, in the same run. During a trial hold the global cap and
exact FFN/head selections unchanged; all scores can still learn. Re-rank hidden
identities each update. Smaller hidden width must strictly reduce actual parameters.
Use paired original and accepted-parent errors. A confirmed routine/development or
fitting regression restores the full pre-trial state: scores, optimizer, sample cursor,
coverage, RNG and priority. Block further hidden reduction after rejection.
An uncertain result receives up to 280 updates at that width. At 280 trial updates,
run full development and native-action checks; accept only passing evidence and
<=0.5% hidden identity churn. Otherwise restore; uncertainty is reported separately
from confirmed harm. The two trials add at most two full checks, no additional
optimizer-update budget or independent model training runs.
After acceptance, FFN/head allocation can reuse capacity under the unchanged cap.
No physical deletion occurs before finalization.

CAPACITY AND STOPPING
Score AdamW LR .01, 50-update warm-up, betas .9/.999, zero decay, gradient clip 1.
Batch 256 = eight distinct GPU workers x microbatch one x 32 accumulation steps.
First 70 updates keep everything. Nominal reduction knots: 14B at schedule 560,
12B at 1,120 and 10B at 1,680. This is a maximum reduction pace, not mandatory sizes.
Every 70 attempted updates fitting-only paired task/noise feedback authorizes at
most the next 70 reduction updates. Require eight episodes per task/noise stratum,
passing all task/suite comparisons and bounded FFN/head/hidden turnover.
Hidden trials, harm and uncertainty hold the size clock; no forced catch-up.
Stop size reduction at attempted update 1,680, even above 10B.
Target finish 1,960; extend once to 2,240 if necessary. Finalization needs five checks
spanning >=280 retained-lineage updates at fixed capacity, hidden identity overlap
>=99.5%, every FFN overlap >=99%, unchanged head identities, active finite gradients,
passing full quality, no unresolved trial, and every fitting window seen >=twice.
Unmet conditions at the limit mean NOT FINALIZED, no automatic compact export.
General quality rescue restores the parent even if count is unchanged but identities
have become harmful. Retained-lineage coverage excludes discarded updates.

QUALITY AND DATA ROLES
Routine monitor: 140 windows every 70 updates. Scheduled full checks: 0,280,560,
1120,1680,1960 and, if needed,2240, plus up to two hidden-trial deadline checks.
Full checks use 1,119 development windows and 140 native ten-step action windows.
Inspect aggregate action error plus position, quaternion-coordinate and gripper
errors separately, for both flow and sampled actions. Also inspect GT future-video
error. Preserve paired original/parent comparisons. Task/suite margins are 5%/2%
relative offline error, not percentage-point success margins. Component family and
reference comparisons have adjusted alpha; repeated adaptive development checks
are screening rules, not an unbiased final statistical guarantee.
The 1,120-window/280-episode audit is opened only after final selection is locked.
Audit metrics do not steer masks and are not an export-selection gate. Its prior use
in unrelated experiments is unverified. Simulation and compact recovery are separate.
